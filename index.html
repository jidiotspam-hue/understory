Comparative Folklore & Narrative Structures in Modern Japanese Media

Department of East Asian Studies • Fall Term • Prof. K. Arisawa

Week 6: Archetypes of the Trickster and the Vow

Analyzing recurring motifs in contemporary animation serials. Focus on contrasting the supernatural contract tropes in post-2010 serialization with classical Heian folkloric traditions.

  • Reading 1: The Quintessential Tropes: Multiple Protagonist Symmetry (pp. 45–82)
  • Reading 2: Taisho-era Demonology and Sword Mythology in Popular Print (pp. 104–130)
  • Reading 3: Subversive Contracts in Urban Dark Fantasy (pp. 210–245)
